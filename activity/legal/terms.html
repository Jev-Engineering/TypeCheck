What TypeCheck does

Experimental service

TypeCheck is experimental. Text analysis and image matching can be wrong in either direction: it can miss content you wanted caught and flag content that is fine. Native text starts disabled and requires explicit configuration and separate text calibration approval. Automatic actions stay off until an administrator approves and enables them. Model probabilities do not establish verified wrongdoing. Don't rely on TypeCheck as your only moderation and review its results.
